This is for travellers who want Kashmir's core experience without stretching into a longer or more expensive circuit. It gives you the essentials in a way that still feels scenic and satisfying.

The focus here is value, not luxury. That means practical hotels, straightforward transport, one strong lake experience, and two of Kashmir's most requested valley outings. It works very well for student groups, quick family escapes, or couples wanting an affordable first trip.

Because the route is shorter, the planning matters even more. The aim is to cut wasted movement and make sure each day still feels like a meaningful part of Kashmir.

Day 1: Arrival and Srinagar check-in

Airport pickup, hotel or houseboat check-in, evening Dal Lake time, and an easy first-night introduction to Srinagar.

Day 2: Gulmarg day trip

Head to Gulmarg for meadow views, Gondola access if desired, and a full day of mountain scenery before returning by evening.

Day 3: Pahalgam experience

Drive to Pahalgam, explore the valley stretch, riverside views, and local market areas, then return or stay depending on final routing.

Day 4: Departure

Transfer to Srinagar Airport with support for shopping stops or meal breaks if schedule allows.
