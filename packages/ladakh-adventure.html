Adventure with acclimatisation built in

This package is not only about covering Ladakh's famous names. It is structured to help travellers actually enjoy them without crashing from altitude, overdriving, or poor pacing.

The plan gives you acclimatisation time in Leh before the harder road sections, then layers the route into Nubra and Pangong with enough time for stops, monastery visits, and lakefront stays. It works best for people who want the classic Ladakh circuit done properly rather than done quickly.

United Arts & Crafts Holidays coordinates this as a support-heavy route because Ladakh is less forgiving than Kashmir. That means clearer transport planning, permit handling, and a practical eye on comfort at altitude.

Day 1: Arrival in Leh and acclimatisation

Airport transfer, hotel check-in, rest, hydration guidance, and only light evening movement to help your body adapt to Ladakh altitude.

Day 2: Leh local circuit

Visit Shanti Stupa, Leh Palace, market lanes, and nearby monasteries while keeping the day manageable for acclimatisation.

Day 3: Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La

Cross one of the world's highest motorable routes and descend into Nubra for dune landscapes, village stops, and a camp or hotel stay.

Day 4: Nubra exploration

Visit Diskit monastery, Hunder dunes, optional camel ride, and enjoy a more open exploration day before the next long drive.

Day 5: Nubra to Pangong

Travel through one of Ladakh's most dramatic road sections to Pangong Lake and stay close to the water for sunset and early morning views.

Day 6: Pangong to Leh

Return to Leh with scenic stopovers, flexible tea breaks, and evening free for shopping or rest.

Day 7: Departure

Airport drop with support for early check-out, meal stops, and flight-time coordination.

This package is best for travellers comfortable with long drives, changing weather, and high altitude. It is not sold as a rushed sightseeing product because Ladakh rewards slower pacing and good support.
