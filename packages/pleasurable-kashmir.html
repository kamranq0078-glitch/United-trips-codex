This is the ideal first Kashmir trip for travellers who want the greatest hits without rushing. It balances iconic lake time, a scenic meadow day, a relaxed valley stay, and one dramatic mountain excursion.

It works especially well for visitors landing in Srinagar for the first time because it keeps the route familiar, scenic, and manageable. You get the classic Kashmir experiences people usually ask for first: Dal Lake, Mughal gardens, Gondola views, valley meadows, and one full road-trip style mountain day.

Because the itinerary is paced carefully, it suits families, couples, and travellers who want strong coverage of Kashmir without feeling like every day is packed from dawn to night.

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar and Dal Lake evening

Meet our driver at Srinagar Airport, transfer to your houseboat or hotel, settle in, and head out for a sunset Shikara ride across Dal Lake. Dinner and overnight in Srinagar.

Day 2: Srinagar sightseeing and Mughal gardens

After breakfast, visit Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, Chashme Shahi, and the old city. Stop for kahwa and handicrafts before returning for a relaxed evening.

Day 3: Gulmarg day trip

Drive to Gulmarg, enjoy the meadows, Gondola experience, and optional snow or pony activities depending on the season. Return to Srinagar by evening.

Day 4: Pahalgam valley experience

Travel to Pahalgam via saffron fields and apple country. Explore Betaab Valley, the Lidder River corridor, and local markets. Overnight in Pahalgam.

Day 5: Sonamarg excursion and departure

After an early breakfast, make a scenic detour toward Sonamarg if flight timing permits, or transfer directly to the airport with one final mountain drive.
