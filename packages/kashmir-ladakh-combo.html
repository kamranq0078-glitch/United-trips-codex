Road Trip Combo

Kashmir + Ladakh Combo

10 Days / 9 Nights · Kashmir valleys + Ladakh passes

₹42,000 / personLong route · high-scenic drive

From soft valley Kashmir to dramatic Ladakh

SrinagarSonamargKargilLehPangong

This is the route for travellers who want one trip to move through two entirely different Himalayan personalities: green Kashmir and stark, high-altitude Ladakh.

It begins with the comfort and familiarity of the Kashmir side, then transitions into the road-trip drama of Sonamarg, Kargil, Leh, Nubra, and Pangong. The route is longer, but it rewards travellers who want contrast, storytelling, and a journey that feels expansive rather than local.

Because the terrain and pacing change sharply across regions, this package is planned more carefully than a standard valley tour. It suits serious road-trip travellers, photographers, and people who want both beauty and scale in one holiday.

Day 1: Arrive in Srinagar

Airport pickup, hotel or houseboat check-in, and quiet evening by the lake.

Day 2: Srinagar local sightseeing

Explore boulevard, Mughal gardens, and heritage stretches while adjusting to the trip rhythm.

Day 3: Gulmarg or free Kashmir day

Use a valley day before the longer mountain route begins.

Day 4: Srinagar to Kargil via Sonamarg

One of the great Himalayan drives, crossing Sonamarg and mountain passes en route to Kargil.

Day 5: Kargil to Leh

Continue through monastery country and dramatic trans-Himalayan scenery into Leh.

Day 6: Leh acclimatisation and local exploration

Visit Leh Palace, market, monasteries, and keep the day moderate for altitude comfort.

Day 7: Leh to Nubra Valley

Cross Khardung La and descend into Nubra for dunes, monasteries, and village stays.

Day 8: Nubra to Pangong

Move through a remote road section into Pangong Lake for evening and sunrise views.

Day 9: Pangong to Leh

Return to Leh for final shopping, rest, and trip close-out.

Day 10: Departure

Airport transfer with support for check-out timing and route coordination.
