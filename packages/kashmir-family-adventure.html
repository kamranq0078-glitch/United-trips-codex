Overview built for real family travel

This route is designed for families who want Kashmir to feel scenic and enjoyable without becoming exhausting. The drive days are balanced, the sightseeing stays familiar, and there is enough downtime for children and older family members.

Instead of pushing a rushed checklist, the itinerary combines the most requested Kashmir experiences with practical comfort: a Dal Lake stay, a full Gulmarg day, a softer valley segment in Pahalgam, and one dramatic mountain drive toward Sonamarg. It is especially good for parents travelling with children or extended families travelling together.

The biggest strength of this package is how flexible it is on the ground. If weather changes, a child gets tired, or the group wants more time in one valley, the route is still easy to adapt without collapsing the full trip.

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar and houseboat check-in

Meet our driver at Srinagar Airport, transfer to your houseboat or family hotel, settle in, and enjoy a calm evening Shikara ride on Dal Lake. Dinner and overnight in Srinagar.

Day 2: Srinagar local sightseeing

After breakfast, visit Mughal gardens, boulevard road viewpoints, and selected old-city stops. The pace stays family-friendly with room for tea breaks, shopping, and lakefront time.

Day 3: Gulmarg day excursion

Drive to Gulmarg for meadow views, Gondola access, pony options, and seasonal snow activities. Return by evening with enough time for dinner and rest.

Day 4: Srinagar to Pahalgam

Travel through saffron and apple-belt stretches toward Pahalgam. Check in, relax beside the Lidder River, and spend the evening at a slower pace.

Day 5: Betaab Valley, Aru Valley and family leisure time

Explore the most popular Pahalgam side valleys, with time for horse riding, riverside walks, and photography. The day is built to feel open rather than tightly scheduled.

Day 6: Sonamarg mountain day

Make a scenic drive toward Sonamarg for glacier views, open meadows, and one of the most dramatic road journeys in the Kashmir circuit. Return or overnight depending on operational flow.

Day 7: Departure support

Transfer back toward Srinagar Airport with shopping or meal stops if timing allows. The team stays available for route changes and departure-day coordination.
