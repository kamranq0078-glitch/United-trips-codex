Our Story

Kashmir's Premier Tour & Travel Specialists.

We started United arts and crafts Holidays with one belief: people visiting Kashmir deserve more than a rushed, generic itinerary. They deserve thoughtful routes, trusted local partners, and the confidence that someone nearby is looking after every moving piece.

We grew up around Dal Lake mornings, Gulmarg winters, and long drives through Pahalgam and Sonamarg. That local memory bank becomes practical value for our guests: better pacing, smarter hotel choices, real-time weather judgment, and access to places that generic online platforms rarely explain well.

Today, we help families, honeymooners, pilgrimage groups, photographers, and adventure seekers experience Kashmir with a calm, premium, editorial feel backed by operational discipline on the ground.
