Services

Travel Services Designed Around Kashmir

United arts and crafts Holidays operates from Khanyar, Srinagar and offers both complete holiday planning and standalone travel services for visitors who need specific local help.

Tour Packages

Complete Kashmir itineraries for couples, families, friend groups, honeymooners, and travellers who want the core Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonamarg circuit handled properly from arrival to departure.

Packages are structured around real drive times, realistic stopovers, hotel suitability, and how each season changes the route experience.

Private Cab Service

AC cab bookings with experienced local drivers for Srinagar city travel, airport pickup and drop, Gulmarg and Pahalgam day trips, and full Kashmir circuit transport.

This works well for travellers who already have stays booked but need dependable local movement without arranging each ride separately.

Houseboat Booking

Budget, deluxe, premium, and super deluxe houseboat stays on Dal Lake and nearby waterfront zones, with help choosing the right comfort level for your trip and budget.

This is especially useful for first-time visitors who want a proper lake stay without guessing between dozens of inconsistent listings.

Hotel Booking

Handpicked hotel stays across Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, Katra, and Ladakh routes for travellers who want verified options rather than random online inventory.

The selection process focuses on matching hotel type to trip purpose, whether that means honeymoon ambience, family practicality, or better budget value.

Shikara Rides

Sunset rides, floating market mornings, and private lake experiences for travellers who want the essential Srinagar moment woven into their trip without last-minute bargaining.

They can be folded into a full package or booked as a focused local experience while you stay in Srinagar.

Adventure Activities

Skiing, Gondola assistance, horse riding, trekking, rafting, ATV rides, and local outdoor add-ons chosen to match the season and your comfort level.

Local guidance matters most here because access, snow, visibility, and activity quality shift strongly by month.

Amarnath Yatra

Pilgrimage package planning with transport support, route coordination, accommodation guidance, and itinerary assistance for travellers who want a smoother yatra experience.

The goal is to simplify a journey that otherwise becomes stressful when permits, timing, and stay logistics are left to the last minute.

Vaishno Devi Packages

Katra-based travel support including transfers, stays, darshan planning, and optional itinerary extensions for travellers combining pilgrimage with Kashmir sightseeing.

It suits families and spiritual travellers who want one coordinated plan instead of separate hotel, transport, and darshan arrangements.
