Is Kashmir Safe to Visit in 2025? Honest Answer

By United Arts & Crafts Holidays Team · Published April 15, 2026

The honest answer is that Kashmir is best approached with practical planning rather than fear or blind optimism. Most travellers have smooth, memorable trips, especially when they work with a local team that monitors routes, weather, and real-time conditions.

What safety means in practice

For travellers, safety usually comes down to choosing the right route, working with reliable drivers, avoiding overpacked schedules, and staying flexible if weather or local conditions shift. These are practical travel concerns, not just headlines.

Who should plan more carefully

Families with children, senior travellers, and anyone combining Kashmir with high-altitude Ladakh routes should prioritise good logistics and realistic pacing.

How to travel more confidently

  • Book with a responsive local operator
  • Keep buffer time in your itinerary
  • Use airport transfers and verified stays
  • Ask for current route advice before you fly
