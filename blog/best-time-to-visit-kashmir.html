Best Time to Visit Kashmir: Month-by-Month Guide 2025

By United Arts & Crafts Holidays Team · Published April 15, 2026

The best time to visit Kashmir depends less on a single perfect month and more on what kind of trip you want. Kashmir changes dramatically across the year, and each season attracts a different kind of traveller.

January to February

These are the core snow months. Gulmarg becomes the star destination, honeymoon travellers love the white landscapes, and road conditions need more active monitoring.

March to April

Early spring brings tulips, fresher air, and the return of greener scenery. This is excellent for travellers who want colour without summer crowds.

May to June

This is one of the easiest windows for families. The weather is comfortable, meadows are open, and most classic routes are fully active.

July to August

Monsoon is lighter in Kashmir than many other Indian destinations, but travel planning still benefits from local advice because hill roads and visibility can vary.

September to October

Autumn brings crisp weather, beautiful foliage, and slightly calmer tourist flow. Many photographers prefer this stretch for its colours and softer light.

November to December

This is the transition into winter. Prices can be attractive in early November, while late December begins the snowfall season again.

Which month should you choose?

  • For snow: January or February
  • For tulips and fresh spring scenery: April
  • For family holidays: May or June
  • For quieter photography: September or October
