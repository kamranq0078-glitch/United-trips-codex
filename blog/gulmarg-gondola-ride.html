Gulmarg Gondola: Everything You Need to Know 2025

By United arts and crafts Holidays Team · Published April 15, 2026

The Gulmarg Gondola is one of the biggest reasons travellers add Gulmarg to their Kashmir itinerary. It is not just a cable car ride. It is a shift in perspective, weather, and altitude that can completely reshape the day.

What to expect

The gondola usually runs in stages, with the upper stage taking you to a more dramatic, weather-sensitive, higher-altitude zone. If you are travelling with children, older parents, or anyone new to altitude, plan conservatively.

Best time to go

In winter, the gondola is part of the snow experience. In summer, it is all about long mountain views and cooler air. Morning slots are usually a better choice for clarity and smoother operations.

What to wear

Layers are essential year-round. In colder months, you will want gloves, a cap, snow boots or rentals, and sunglasses.
