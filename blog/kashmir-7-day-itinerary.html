Kashmir in 7 Days: The Perfect First-Timer's Itinerary

By United Arts & Crafts Holidays Team · Published April 15, 2026

If you are visiting Kashmir for the first time, seven days gives you enough space to see the iconic highlights without turning the trip into a series of exhausting drives. The best itinerary mixes city culture, lake time, meadows, mountain roads, and at least one day that feels completely unhurried.

Day 1: Arrive in Srinagar

Land in Srinagar, settle into your hotel or houseboat, and keep the first day deliberately easy. A sunset Shikara ride is the perfect reset after a travel day and gives you your first real sense of Dal Lake's pace and beauty.

Day 2: Srinagar sightseeing

Use the second day to explore Mughal gardens, the old city, local handicrafts, and a slow lakeside evening. It is better to enjoy Srinagar properly than to treat it as a transit stop.

Day 3: Gulmarg day trip or overnight

Gulmarg is where first-timers get the big mountain drama. In winter it is snow and skiing; in summer it is open green slopes and long views. If you are short on time, a day trip works. If you want a more premium pace, stay overnight.

Day 4 and 5: Pahalgam

Pahalgam adds river scenery, pine forests, and family-friendly pacing. Spend one day on the transfer and local exploration, then use the next for Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, or a quieter riverside afternoon. This part of the itinerary helps balance the more active sightseeing days.

Day 6: Sonamarg or a flexible day

Keep day six flexible. If weather is good and roads are open, Sonamarg makes for an unforgettable glacier and mountain-road experience. If you prefer a slower trip, use the day for shopping, an extra Srinagar night, or a premium add-on like a heritage walk or curated food stop.

Day 7: Departure

Plan a calm airport transfer day. Kashmir road timings can shift with traffic, weather, and season, so a local operator is valuable here.

Budget and packing basics

  • Budget travellers can make this route work with 3-star stays and shared elements.
  • Families usually prefer private cab flexibility.
  • Carry layers even in summer because mornings and evenings can turn cool.
  • Good walking shoes matter more than heavy luggage.

FAQ

Is 7 days enough for Kashmir?

Yes. It is the sweet spot for a first-time trip because you can cover the signature destinations without feeling rushed.

What is the most important booking tip?
