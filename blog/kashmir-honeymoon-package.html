Kashmir Honeymoon: Complete Planning Guide 2025

By United Arts and Crafts Holiday Team · Published April 15, 2026

Kashmir works beautifully for couples because it offers romance without forcing the trip into a single mood. You can combine elegant lake evenings, snowy landscapes, long drives, boutique stays, and quiet valley time in one itinerary.

Best honeymoon destinations

Srinagar is ideal for the classic lakeside romance, Gulmarg adds mountain drama and snow, and Pahalgam introduces a calmer, more private rhythm. This three-stop route gives most couples exactly the balance they are looking for.

Best duration

Five to seven days is perfect. Shorter than that feels rushed, while longer itineraries should usually add slower pacing rather than more destination-hopping.

Best season

Choose April to June for greenery, flowers, and open roads. Choose December to February if your dream honeymoon includes snowfall and winter photography.

Romantic add-ons worth paying for

  • One boutique stay rather than only standard hotels
  • A quality houseboat for one night
  • Private Shikara ride at sunset
  • A candlelit dinner or room decor setup
