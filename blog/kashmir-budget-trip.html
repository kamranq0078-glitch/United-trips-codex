Kashmir Budget Trip: ₹15,000 for 5 Days

By United Trips Team · Published April 15, 2026

A Kashmir budget trip is absolutely possible if you focus on route efficiency, practical hotel choices, and one or two standout experiences instead of trying to buy every activity. The goal is not to make the trip cheap at the cost of comfort. It is to spend smartly.

What a realistic budget includes

  • Simple, clean accommodation
  • Breakfast-included stays
  • Shared or well-planned transport
  • One premium memory like a Shikara ride

Where travellers overspend

The most common mistakes are too many hotel changes, impulse activity spending in Gulmarg, and poor transport planning that leads to higher last-minute rates.

Best route for a budget trip

A compact Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam route usually gives the best value. It covers the essentials without forcing high-distance road costs.
