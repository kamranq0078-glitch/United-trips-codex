Leh Ladakh from Kashmir: The Ultimate Road Trip Guide

By United Trips Team · Published April 15, 2026

The Kashmir to Ladakh route is one of the most rewarding road journeys in the Himalayas. It starts with lake city comfort and green valleys, then shifts gradually toward raw altitude, monasteries, high passes, and huge desert-like landscapes.

Why this route is special

It gives your body a more gradual transition into altitude compared with some faster fly-in itineraries. That makes it attractive for travellers who want the road-trip story as much as the Ladakh destination itself.

What to plan for

  • Acclimatisation time
  • Long but scenic drive days
  • Permit paperwork and local rules
  • Smart overnight stops

Ideal trip length

Seven to ten days is the minimum useful range. Less than that usually creates unnecessary fatigue.
