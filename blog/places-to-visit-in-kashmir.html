Top 10 Places to Visit in Kashmir (2025 Guide)

By United Arts & Crafts Holidays Team · Published April 15, 2026

Kashmir is not one single landscape. It is a sequence of moods: lake city, pine valley, snow meadow, glacier road, alpine meadow, and high-altitude desert. That is why the best trips combine destinations rather than overcommitting to just one.

  1. Srinagar: for Dal Lake, houseboats, old city atmosphere, and gardens.
  2. Gulmarg: for Gondola rides, snowfall, and dramatic mountain views.
  3. Pahalgam: for river valleys, soft scenery, and family-friendly pacing.
  4. Sonamarg: for glacier routes and big Himalayan road-trip energy.
  5. Leh Ladakh: for travellers extending into high-altitude adventure.
  6. Yusmarg: for quieter alpine meadows.
  7. Gurez Valley: for offbeat, deeply scenic travel.
  8. Doodhpathri: for a lighter, refreshing day trip.
  9. Aru Valley: for calm landscapes near Pahalgam.
  10. Betaab Valley: for classic postcard Kashmir scenery.

How to choose the right mix

First-timers should focus on Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, and Sonamarg. Repeat travellers can add Yusmarg, Doodhpathri, or Gurez for a stronger sense of discovery.
